# Astropanth's knowledge basis + recommended Marathi books for a practitioner

## Part 1 — What knowledge Astropanth is built on

Important distinction: Astropanth does **not** copy text from any book. It's an
**engine** — it *calculates* everything from first principles and only uses a
small, curated interpretation layer. So the "sources" are the classical
*systems/rules* it implements, validated numerically against trusted almanacs.

**Astrology (Vedic, sidereal):**
- **Lahiri ayanamsa** sidereal positions from a self-hosted, Swiss-Ephemeris-grade
  engine — verified to the arc-minute against **DrikPanchang** and **ProKerala**
  (see `store-assets/ACCURACY_VERIFICATION.md`).
- Classical **Parashari** framework: Rashi (signs), Bhava (houses), Graha
  (planets), **Vimshottari Dasha** (120-year cycle, 9 lords), **Panchang** (the
  five angas — Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Vara), **Sade Sati** (Saturn from
  natal Moon). These trace to the standard canon: **Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
  (BPHS)**, **Phaladeepika**, **Saravali**.
- **Kundli matching** via the classical **Ashtakoota Gun Milan** (36 gunas across
  8 kootas) + **Mangal Dosha** — the standard matching system.

**Numerology:**
- **Birth / Destiny / Name numbers** (Pythagorean-style digit reduction),
  **Kua number** (Feng Shui / Eight Mansions), and the **Lo Shu grid** (Chinese
  numerology). All deterministic arithmetic.

**Interpretation text:** a curated in-house knowledge base (built during the
knowledge-compendium work), kept grounded so answers stay classical and specific
rather than generic AI output.

**Vastu:** not in the app today — it's a possible future vertical (competitors
like arviendsud lead with it).

If you want the *primary canon* a serious Vedic practitioner keeps regardless of
language: **BPHS**, **Phaladeepika** (Mantreswara), **Saravali** (Kalyana Varma),
and **Jataka Parijata** for astrology; **Muhurta** texts for electional work.

## Part 2 — Best Marathi books for a practitioner

These are well-regarded and currently available (Amazon.in / Marathi bookstores).
"Best" in astrology is somewhat subjective — check the current edition and reviews
before buying, and prefer established authors/publishers (e.g. Saket Prakashan).

### Astrology / ज्योतिष

- **भारतीय ज्योतिषशास्त्र — शंकर बाळकृष्ण दीक्षित (Shankar Balkrishna Dikshit).**
  The landmark Marathi scholarly work on Indian astronomy + astrology (compiled
  from 500+ Sanskrit texts). Deep, reference-grade — the serious foundation, not a
  quick primer.
- **ग्रहांगण / Kaalnirnay lineage — Jayant Salgaonkar.** The most respected
  practical Maharashtrian astrology/panchang tradition; excellent for real-world,
  culturally-grounded practice.
- **Learning-level primers** (Amazon's Marathi Jyotish section) by authors such as
  **V. G. Nawathe, Dr. Vikas Khilare, Gaurish Borkar** — useful stepping stones
  before the classical texts. Skim reviews and pick one that matches your level.
- **Classical translations:** Marathi/Hindi editions of **Brihat Parashara Hora
  Shastra** and **Phaladeepika** — get these once you're past the basics; they're
  the actual practitioner canon.

### Numerology / अंकशास्त्र

- **अंकशास्त्र — राजेंद्र रा. पवार (Rajendra Ra. Pawar)** (2023 paperback) — a
  current, approachable practical guide.
- **सिद्धांतिक अंकशास्त्र — मेघश्याम पाठक (Meghashyam Pathak)** — more theoretical/
  systematic, good for building fundamentals.
- **अंकशास्त्र — पुरुषोत्तम वारवतकर (Purushottam Varvatkar)** — a widely-listed
  Marathi title.
- (eBooks: Pimple Jagannath, Darshan Shukla's "जन्म-तारीख आणि मिसिंग अंक" for the
  missing-number / Lo-Shu angle.)
- Western roots worth knowing: **Cheiro** and **Sepharial** (the classic
  numerology sources most modern books build on).

### Vastu Shastra / वास्तुशास्त्र

- **VastuRaviraj — डॉ. रविराज आहिरराव (Dr. Raviraj Ahirrao)**, esp. *"वास्तुशास्त्र:
  सुख-शांती आणि समृद्धीसाठी"* (Saket Prakashan). The most prominent modern Marathi
  Vastu author — practitioner-oriented, widely used.
- **सुलभ वास्तुशास्त्र — रघुनाथ श्रीपाद देशपांडे (Raghunath Shripad Deshpande).** A
  clear, classic Marathi primer (also on the Internet Archive).
- Classical depth (Sanskrit/English): **Mayamatam** and **Vishwakarma Vastushastra**
  once you want the source treatises.

---

### Suggested practitioner path
Start with one practical Marathi primer per subject → move to Salgaonkar/Ahirrao
for grounded practice → then the classical canon (BPHS/Phaladeepika, Mayamatam)
for depth. For astrology specifically, Dikshit's *भारतीय ज्योतिषशास्त्र* is the
prestige reference to grow into.
