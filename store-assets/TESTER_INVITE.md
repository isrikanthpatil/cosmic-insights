# Astropanth — closed test invite (engagement-focused)

Google rejected the first production application because the closed test didn't show **12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days AND actively engaging** with the app. This version of the invite fixes that: it asks testers to actually *use* the app and *stay opted in*.

**Before sending:**
- Push the latest build to the closed-testing track (matching, More tab, notifications, polish) so testers have real features to try.
- In Play Console → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing → **Testers**, copy the **opt-in link** and paste it below where it says [OPT-IN LINK].
- Make sure each person's exact Gmail is in your tester list.
- You need **12+ people** who install AND remain opted in for 14 continuous days.

---

## Message to send (WhatsApp / email)

Hi! I've built an astrology + numerology app called **Astropanth** and I need a few testers before I can launch it on the Play Store. Could you help for ~2 weeks? It only takes a few minutes total. 🙏

**Step 1 — Join the test (Android phone):**
1. Open this link and tap **"Become a tester"**: [OPT-IN LINK]
2. Then install **Astropanth** from Google Play.
   (Use the same Google account / email you gave me, or it won't let you in.)

**Step 2 — Actually try it (this part really matters):**
Please open the app a couple of times over the next two weeks and try these:
- Enter your birth details for your free reading
- Look at your **Astrology** tab (Sun/Moon/Rising, strengths, remedies)
- Check your **Numerology** and the Lo Shu grid
- Ask a question or two in **AskAstro**
- Try **Kundli Matching** under the **More** tab (match two birth charts)
- Optionally turn on the daily horoscope reminder in Profile → Settings

**Step 3 — Send quick feedback:**
Tell me anything — what you liked, anything confusing or broken. You can reply to me directly, or leave private feedback in the Play Store (scroll to the bottom of the app's Play page → "Send feedback").

**⚠️ One important thing:** please **stay a tester (keep it installed) for the full 2 weeks** — if you leave the test or uninstall, it resets my launch clock. Thank you so much, this genuinely makes the launch possible! 🌟

## Why engagement matters (for you, the developer)
Google explicitly rejects applications where testers "aren't engaged." So:
- Aim for **more than 12** testers (buffer against drop-offs).
- Nudge them mid-window ("could you open it once more and try Kundli Matching?").
- Read/act on feedback in Play Console → Ratings and reviews → **Testing feedback**.
- Keep a note of feedback themes — you'll summarize them when you re-apply for production.

## After 14 continuous days with 12+ engaged testers
Play Console → Dashboard → **Apply for production** → answer the 3 sections (about the closed test, the app, production readiness). Ask me and I'll draft those answers with you.
