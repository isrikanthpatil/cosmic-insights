# Privacy Policy

Astropanth · Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Astropanth ("we", "us", the "App") provides personalised astrology and numerology
insights based on your birth details. This policy explains what information we
collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We process your personal data
with your consent, which you give when you create an account and provide your birth
details, and which you can withdraw at any time (see "Your rights").

## Information we collect

- **Account information** — your email address and name when you create an account
  or sign in with Google or Apple.
- **Birth details** — your date of birth, time of birth (optional), place of birth,
  and gender. We use these solely to calculate your astrology chart and numerology
  numbers.
- **Questions you ask** — the messages you send to the AskAstro feature, so we can
  generate a response.
- **Limited technical data** — your approximate IP address, used only to apply rate
  limits (for example, the free daily question limit for guests). We do not build
  advertising profiles.

You can browse the astrology and numerology sections as a guest without creating an
account. In guest mode, birth details you enter are stored only on your device.

## How we use your information

- To generate your astrology and numerology readings and daily/weekly horoscopes.
- To power the AskAstro chat assistant.
- To create and secure your account and send account-related emails (such as
  verification and password resets).
- To protect the service from abuse.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party
advertising.

## Service providers and international transfer

We use a small number of trusted providers to operate the App:

- **Google / Apple** — if you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple",
  they provide us your name and email to create your account.
- **AI processing** — the question you type into AskAstro, together with your chart
  summary, is sent to an AI language-model provider to generate a reply. It is used
  only to produce your answer.
- **Email delivery (Brevo)** — used to send account emails such as verification and
  password-reset links.
- **Hosting** — our application and database run on our own servers hosted with a
  cloud infrastructure provider.

Some of these providers are located **outside India**, so your personal data may be
processed internationally. We use reputable providers and rely on lawful transfer
mechanisms; such transfers are permitted under India's Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023, except to any territory the Government of India may restrict.

## Data storage and security

Your account data is stored in our own database on secured servers, transmitted over
encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never
visible to us. While no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, we take
reasonable measures to protect your information. In the event of a personal data
breach that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection
Board of India as required by law.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. You can
permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from
*Profile → Settings → Delete Account* in the App, or at
https://www.astropanth.com/delete-account. You may also email us to request deletion.

## Children

Astropanth is intended for users aged **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect
personal data from anyone under 18. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023, processing a child's personal data requires verifiable parental or guardian
consent; because our service is directed to adults, we do not knowingly do so. If
you believe someone under 18 has provided us personal data, please contact us and we
will delete it.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, update and erase your personal data, and to
**withdraw your consent** at any time. You also have the right to **nominate** another
individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity. To
exercise any of these rights, use the in-app controls or contact us at the address
below.

## Grievance redressal

If you have any concern or complaint about how we handle your personal data, you can
contact our Grievance Officer at **support@astropanth.com**. We will acknowledge and
address grievances within the timelines required under applicable law. You may also
escalate a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by
updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, notifying you in the App.

## Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@astropanth.com.

Astropanth provides insights for guidance and self-reflection and does not offer
medical, financial, or legal advice.
