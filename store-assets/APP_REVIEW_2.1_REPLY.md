Thank you for reviewing Astropanth. Please find the requested information below.

**2. App purpose and target audience**
Astropanth is a Vedic (sidereal / Lahiri) astrology and numerology app for users
in India. From a person's birth details it generates their birth chart (Kundli) —
Sun, Moon and Ascendant signs, planetary positions and houses — plus daily and
weekly horoscopes (Rashifal), the daily Panchang, Kundli matching (Ashtakoota Gun
Milan) for marriage compatibility, Sade Sati and Dasha periods, and a full
numerology reading (Birth, Destiny and Kua numbers with a Lo Shu grid). It also
includes "AskAstro", an AI guide that answers astrology and numerology questions
grounded in the user's own chart. The target audience is adults in India
interested in Vedic astrology and numerology. The app is available in English,
Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu, and is intended for guidance and
self-reflection only — it is not a substitute for professional advice.

**4. External services used**
- Backend: a self-hosted PocketBase server (api.astropanth.com) for accounts,
  saved profiles and birth-place lookup.
- Astrology engine: a self-hosted Swiss Ephemeris (Lahiri sidereal) calculation
  engine — all astrological calculations are performed by us, not a third party.
- AI: Groq (LLM) powers the AskAstro chat; its answers are restricted to
  astrology/numerology and grounded in the user's chart.
- Email: Brevo (account verification and password-reset emails).
- Authentication: Sign in with Apple and Google OAuth.
- There is no payment processor active on iOS — this version of the app is free
  and contains no in-app purchases.

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions consistently across all regions; there are no region-locked
features. The content is India-focused Vedic astrology and is offered in six
languages. This version is distributed for India.

**6. Regulated industry / protected material**
Astrology and numerology are not regulated industries. All astrological and
numerological content is original, produced by our own curated knowledge base and
calculation engine; no protected third-party material is used. The app clearly
states throughout that it is for guidance and self-reflection and is not a
substitute for medical, financial or legal advice.
