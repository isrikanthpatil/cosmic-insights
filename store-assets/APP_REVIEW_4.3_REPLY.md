## If Apple maintains the rejection
- **File a formal appeal** to the App Review Board (Resolution Center → the appeal
  link, or developer.apple.com/contact → App Review → Submit an appeal). The appeal
  lets you argue the 4.3 decision to a different team.
- Consider **requesting a phone call** with App Review (offered via Contact Us) —
  live differentiation conversations sometimes help on 4.3.
- Longer-term levers if it stays blocked: publishing under an **Organization**
  account (a registered entity tends to fare better than a brand-new individual
  account in saturated categories), and continuing to add distinctive features.
- Note: Apple's message suggesting a "web app" is effectively their softer "this
  may not belong as a native app" — not a required path. Your Android app and web
  presence are unaffected either way.
