# Pricing

**Astropanth is free to explore.** Your Kundli chart, daily Rashifal, Panchang,
Kundli matching, numerology, the numerology calculators and AskAstro are all
available at no cost.

Optional paid upgrades are purchased **inside the Astropanth app on Google Play** —
the exact price is always shown in the app before you confirm, and payment and any
applicable taxes are handled by Google Play.
